# Product Contract

## 1. Product Definition

HAPPY is a programme operations platform covering:

`Demand → Supply → Matching → Placement → Outcome`

Enablers operate across the flow:

`M&E / MERL + Marketing + Management / PM`

## 2. Demand Responsibilities

- employer acquisition and engagement
- employer contacts
- programme explanation
- partnership/MOU workflow
- vacancy acquisition
- requirements clarification
- employer-side changes
- recruiter handoff
- employer follow-up

## 3. Supply Responsibilities

- participant mobilization
- minimum intake
- onboarding completion
- consent
- CV collection/creation
- data completeness
- readiness
- candidate pool development
- supply-gap response

## 4. Matching Responsibilities

- review against vacancy/ICP
- pre-screen
- qualify
- submit
- coordinate interviews
- record employer outcome
- confirm start
- verify first income
- confirm placement

## 5. M&E / MERL Responsibilities

- post-placement follow-up
- current employment verification
- retention
- outcome capture
- data-quality review
- impact-story identification
- programme evidence

## 6. Marketing Responsibilities

- campaign requests
- recruitment campaigns
- training campaigns
- content production/review/publishing
- approved impact stories
- programme-attribution reporting where feasible

## 7. Management / PM Responsibilities

- target vs actual vs gap
- cross-workstream risk
- bottlenecks
- programme performance
- approved reports
- exception oversight

## 8. Universal UX Concepts

Every operational record should expose, where relevant:
- status
- owner
- team
- priority
- next action
- due date
- last activity
- history
- tasks
- documents
- notes
- related records

## 9. Role vs Permission

**Role determines the default workspace and information emphasis.**  
**Permission determines what the user may actually see or do.**

## 10. Formal Entity Names

Desktop/system terminology:
- Participants
- Vacancies
- Employers
- Placements
- Outcomes
- Campaigns

Mobile labels may shorten:
- Participants → People
- Vacancies → Jobs

The page title should retain the formal entity name.
